# Privacy Policy for LinkedIn Comment Counter

Last updated: September 2, 2026

LinkedIn Comment Counter ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension operates regarding user data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
LinkedIn Comment Counter does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, browsing history, user credentials, or personal information.

## 2. Local Storage
All statistics generated by the extension (daily, weekly, and monthly comment counts) are stored strictly locally on your device using Google Chrome's secure `chrome.storage.local` API. This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to any remote server, third-party service, or database.

## 3. Host Permissions
The extension requests host permission for `https://www.linkedin.com/*` solely to:
- Detect when you click the comment submit button on LinkedIn.
- Display the floating comment counter user interface widget on your screen.

## 4. Analytics & Tracking
We do NOT use any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics), tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or remote code.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer.
